About the Forum

A platform for collaboration, representation and creative enterprise.

The MCACF booklet describes a community-driven platform established in 2018, with a new committee elected in December 2024 and registration as a Non-Profit Company in May 2025. The Forum brings artists together around shared projects and partnerships.

◎

Representation

Bring the needs of local artists and cultural practitioners into cluster councils, consultations and the annual Summit.

↗

Professionalisation

Support capacity building, admin assistance, templates, reporting and information that helps creatives organise their work.

◇

Partnerships & investment

Connect grassroots creative structures with municipal, institutional, corporate and community partners.

Governance note: The supplied members’ guidebook labels some rules and leadership guidance as proposed for member adoption. This website presents them as guidebook material and does not replace formally adopted policies or resolutions.

Eight sectoral commissions

Commission labels and descriptions below follow the supplied 2026–2030 booklet.

Performing Arts

Theatre, music, dance, festivals and storytelling.

Visual Arts & Crafts

Fine art, photography, sculpture and modern craft.

Audio-Visual & Interactive Media

Film, radio, podcasts and games.

Languages & Publishing

Literature, poetry, archives and language services.

Design, Creative & ACH Services

Fashion, textile, graphics, stage and sound.

Cultural & Natural Heritage

Museums, monuments and heritage tourism.

Arts Education & Training

TVET, CATHSSETA and master-apprentice models.

Indigenous Wisdom

Indigenous knowledge, healing and botanical heritage.

The booklet also refers to a Music Cluster and registration with SAMRO, RiSA and CAPASSO for royalty protection. Confirm the formal commission structure with the Forum before publishing adopted governance rules.

Become part of the network

Membership that connects creatives to opportunity.

Membership benefits listed in the booklet include networking, event support, funding information and referrals, admin assistance, capacity building and a voice in Forum activities. Benefits depend on available resources and do not guarantee funding.

Road to investment

Creative sector dialogue and investment.

The supplied Investment Summit Working Plan identifies a programme dated 4 December 2026, designed to bridge grassroots creative structures with corporate and municipal investment partners.

08:00–11:00

Cluster & sector dialogue

Stakeholder breakfast and introductory engagements with ward representatives, creative cluster heads and sector delegates.

11:00–14:30

Investment presentations & pitches

Structured pitches to public and private sector investors, followed by a networking lunch.

15:00–late

Stakeholder gala dinner & comedy night

A showcase programme intended to mobilise operational capital for ongoing creative projects.

Event information: The supplied working plan lists Kagiso Recreational Centre as the target venue. Confirm final event arrangements with the Forum before publishing this as a confirmed public invitation.
